MINDBLOOM DEMO VIDEO SCRIPT
============================
Title: MindBloom - Student Mental Wellness Tracker | FirstCommit 2026 Hackathon Demo
Duration: 4 minutes

[OPENING - 0:00-0:30]
[Screen: Animated MindBloom logo with sprout emoji]

Narrator: "Hey everyone! I'm excited to present MindBloom — a mental wellness tracker 
built specifically for students. Mental health is a crisis among young people today, 
and I wanted to build something that makes daily wellness checks fun, consistent, and 
meaningful. Let me show you what I built."

[PROBLEM STATEMENT - 0:30-1:00]
[Screen: Split screen - stressed student vs MindBloom dashboard]

Narrator: "1 in 3 students experiences anxiety or depression. Most wellness apps feel 
clinical and boring. MindBloom changes that by gamifying wellness — earning XP, 
building streaks, and leveling up through healthy habits."

[FEATURE 1: MOOD LOGGING - 1:00-1:30]
[Screen: Mood log page - selecting emoji, tagging activities, entering note]

Narrator: "Every day, you can log your mood on a 10-point scale with emoji buttons. 
You can tag what you were doing — studying, exercising, hanging with friends — and add 
notes. Each log earns you 20 XP. Build a streak and get bonus XP!"

[FEATURE 2: JOURNAL - 1:30-2:00]
[Screen: Journal page - creating a new entry]

Narrator: "The private journal gives you a judgment-free space to reflect. Add mood 
tags to your entries, track your word count, and earn 15 XP for each entry. All your 
thoughts are private to you."

[FEATURE 3: BREATHING EXERCISE - 2:00-2:30]
[Screen: Breathing page - animated circle breathing in/out]

Narrator: "Feeling stressed? The guided breathing exercises use proven techniques like 
the 4-7-8 breath to instantly calm your nervous system. Watch the circle — breathe in 
as it expands, hold, then exhale as it shrinks. Sessions are logged and earn you XP!"

[FEATURE 4: CHALLENGES - 2:30-3:00]
[Screen: Challenges page - browsing and joining challenges]

Narrator: "Wellness challenges make habits stick. Join the 7-Day Mood Tracker, the 
Digital Detox, or the Sleep Champion challenge. Each day you complete your challenge, 
mark your progress. Finish and earn up to 200 XP!"

[FEATURE 5: DASHBOARD & ANALYTICS - 3:00-3:30]
[Screen: Dashboard with stats, mood trend chart, level progress]

Narrator: "The dashboard shows your streak, average mood, journal entries, and level 
progress. The mood trend chart lets you spot patterns — maybe you're consistently low 
on Monday mornings. Knowledge is power!"

[TECHNICAL OVERVIEW - 3:30-4:00]
[Screen: Code editor showing backend, then frontend]

Narrator: "Under the hood: the backend is Node.js with Express, using SQLite for 
persistent storage — no cloud database needed! JWT authentication keeps accounts secure. 
The frontend is pure vanilla JavaScript — no React, no Vue — just clean, fast code 
with beautiful CSS animations. It's deployed on Fly.io and lives at 
mindbloom-wellness.fly.dev."

[CLOSING - 3:50-4:00]
Narrator: "Mental health matters. MindBloom is my first attempt to do something about 
it. I learned so much building this — REST APIs, database design, JWT auth, Docker, 
responsive CSS. Thank you FirstCommit for this opportunity. Let's bloom together! 🌱"

[END SCREEN: mindbloom-wellness.fly.dev | github.com/peddipedderu/mindbloom-wellness]
